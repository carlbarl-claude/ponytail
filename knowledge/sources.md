# Official source materials — download these yourself

The College Board publishes released free-response questions (FRQs), official scoring
guidelines, and **real scored student samples with scoring commentary** for free. This
bot does **not** redistribute that copyrighted content. Instead, download the PDFs you
want for your own studying and add them through the bot's upload feature (attach a PDF
in Discord), or drop them into `knowledge/ap_euro/uploads/` or
`knowledge/ap_seminar/uploads/` and run `/reindex`.

Please use these for personal study, and respect the College Board's terms of use —
don't bulk-scrape or redistribute the materials.

## AP European History
- **Exam page (past FRQs + scoring guidelines):**
  https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-european-history/exam
- Each year's page includes the DBQ, LEQ, and SAQs, the **scoring guidelines**, and
  **sample responses with scoring commentary** (the "why this earned its score"
  documents — the most valuable thing to feed the bot).
- **Course and Exam Description (CED)** — full rubrics and skill descriptions:
  search "AP European History Course and Exam Description" on apcentral.

## AP Seminar
- **Exam & performance-task page:**
  https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-seminar/exam
- Includes released end-of-course exam questions, scoring guidelines, and available
  sample responses. AP Seminar releases less material than the history courses because
  much of the grade comes from through-course performance tasks.
- **Course and Exam Description (CED)** — full performance-task rubrics: search
  "AP Seminar and AP Research Course and Exam Description" on apcentral.

## What to prioritize downloading
For the best grounding, get the **scored sample responses + scoring commentary** for a
few recent years. Those show real student work and exactly why each response earned
its points — which is what lets the bot say "your paragraph looks like the sample that
earned a 4, and here's the one thing that separated it from a 5."

## How to add them
1. Download the PDF(s) from the links above.
2. Either **attach the PDF in a Discord message to the bot** (easiest), or copy it into
   the right `uploads/` folder and use the `/reindex` command.
3. Ask your question — the bot will now cite those materials when relevant.
